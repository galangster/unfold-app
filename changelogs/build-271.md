# Build 271 (1.1.6) - Large text layouts and voice onboarding answers

Everything in build 267, plus the two candidates below.

## New

- **Voice answers in onboarding** - The About me field offers an optional microphone. Record, listen, transcribe, edit, and choose Use this answer. Typed text stays intact until you accept.
- **Large text layouts** - Settings, Bible preferences, Library tabs, Companion, onboarding, paywall, notes, and sheets now grow, wrap, and scroll with enlarged system text instead of clipping.

## What to test

- [ ] Enable Settings > Accessibility > Larger Text at the largest size. Open Unfold Settings and confirm every label reads whole and the page scrolls.
- [ ] With large text, open the paywall and confirm plans and reviews scroll above the purchase buttons.
- [ ] With large text, open Companion and confirm suggestions scroll above the message quota and composer.
- [ ] Complete onboarding with large text and confirm narrative, choices, and status text stay reachable.
- [ ] In onboarding About me, tap the microphone, record, transcribe, edit, and choose Use this answer.
- [ ] Cancel a recording mid-way and confirm typed text is unchanged.
