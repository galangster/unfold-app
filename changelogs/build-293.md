Build 293 (1.1.13). Replaces 292 on the same version. Everything since the live 1.1.12 (build 289). Please try:

1. Devotional tab: tap your current book's cover, then swipe its page. The book should turn open onto the reading in one motion, with no curl, no blank frame, and no stretched cover.
2. At the end of a reading, answer the first reflection question and tap Next question. The next field should open and take focus. Tap Done on the last one.
3. Start writing a Today reflection just before the next day's reading arrives (or when a notification banner appears). Your draft should stay in place.
4. Tap a midday check-in notification after 5 p.m. It should open the check-in sheet, not plain Today. Tap any notification with the app fully closed; the splash should clear within a few seconds.
5. Evening: with the wind-down reminder on, you should receive one banner at your evening time, not two with the same text.
6. Profile → Past Devotionals: your first devotional should still be there, labeled as your first, after starting a series.
7. Share a book cover: pick a background, share, reopen the sheet, and confirm it remembers your choice.
8. Turn on the largest text size: Bible headings, the journal, and the announcement pages should stay readable.
9. Finish a reading and look for the Today ambience. You may see a night sky or a mountain river among the approved scenes. You should not see the clouds scene.
10. While a reading is being written, Today should show the calm waiting state with rings, and Go to Today should stay in place on the generating screen.

Known limits: Android and physical-device timing were not tested for this build.
