Build 295 (1.1.13). Replaces canceled 294. Do not install 294. Everything since the live 1.1.12 (build 289). Please try:

1. On iOS 27: install this build, open Unfold, and leave it in the foreground. It should stay open. It should not return to the home screen by itself. Cold-launch it again from a fully closed state.
2. Start a reading before midnight and finish after midnight. It should count as the night you started. The next calendar day's reading should still be unused. Midday check-in and evening wind-down should behave the same way.
3. After a missed or skipped evening, open Companion and take the scripture follow-up it offered. It should return the passage, not fail silently.
4. Devotional tab: tap your current book's cover, then swipe its page. The book should turn open onto the reading in one motion, with no curl, no blank frame, and no stretched cover.
5. At the end of a reading, answer the first reflection question and tap Next question. The next field should open and take focus. Tap Done on the last one.
6. Start writing a Today reflection just before the next day's reading arrives (or when a notification banner appears). Your draft should stay in place.
7. Tap a midday check-in notification after 5 p.m. It should open the check-in sheet, not plain Today. Tap any notification with the app fully closed; the splash should clear within a few seconds.
8. Evening: with the wind-down reminder on, you should receive one banner at your evening time, not two with the same text.
9. Leave Unfold closed for a day, then confirm midday and evening check-ins still arrive. The local horizon stays 14 days, not 21.
10. Profile → Past Devotionals: your first devotional should still be there, labeled as your first, after starting a series.
11. Share a book cover: pick a background, share, reopen the sheet, and confirm it remembers your choice.
12. Turn on the largest text size: Bible headings, the journal, and the announcement pages should stay readable.
13. Finish a reading and look for the Today ambience. You may see a night sky or a mountain river among the approved scenes. You should not see the clouds scene.
14. Open Today and watch the companion: idle motion should start immediately, and the eyes should stay with the sphere.
15. In a companion reply that includes a verse pill, the words around the pill should still read as a sentence. The composer should sit centered.
16. While a reading is being written, Today should show the calm waiting state with rings, and Go to Today should stay in place on the generating screen.
