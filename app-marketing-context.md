## Value Proposition
- **Problem:** Many Bible and devotional apps feel generic, shallow, or disconnected from the user’s real season of life.
- **Target Audience:** Christians who want a more personal, beautiful, reflective daily Bible and devotional experience with journaling and habit support.
- **Unique Differentiator:** Unfold aims to feel like the most personal Bible experience in the world — guided devotionals, scripture reflection, journaling, and habit loop surfaces shaped around the user’s season and needs.
- **Elevator Pitch:** A deeply personal Bible study and devotional app that helps you meet God in Scripture each day through guided reflection, journaling, and a beautiful daily rhythm.
