## What was built

- `rive-src/lib/svg_rml.py`: exact conversion of Illustrator outlined-stroke SVGs (paths with
  cubic handles, polygons, rotated rects, circles, gradients with transforms, clip-paths).
- `rive-src/lib/life_rml.py`: keyframed life (shimmer, TrimPath glints in a GroupEffect,
  streaks, flocks with wing-beat bouts, bokeh, stars). No Luau: the CLI is signed out and
  unsigned scripts do not run in production runtimes.
- `rive-src/lib/unfold_rml.py`: `sway` seam fix and dedupe, `cycle_at`, `DUR % period`
  assertions, `transparent_stop`, glow breathing, `Scene.register` with `rest_pose`.
- `rive-src/scenes/moon-stars/` and `rive-src/scenes/mountain-river/`: `art.svg` + `gen.py`
  (`scene.rml` and `build/` are generated, ignored).
- App: `today-moon-stars.riv` and `today-mountain-river.riv` in `assets/rive/`, the two
  options in `today-ambient-rive.ts`, sources mapped in `AmbientArtCanvas.tsx`, the four
  scene-enumerating tests, `assets/rive/README.md`, and `src/app/qa-rive-ambience.tsx`
  (dev-only preview; allowlisted with a `scene` param limited to `RIVE_AMBIENCE_OPTIONS`).
- Doctrine: `rive-src/DESIGN-REFERENCE.md` gained the designer-SVG section and the approval
  rule.
