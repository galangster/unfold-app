# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Unfold is for people who want a personal, spiritually grounded Bible study/devotional practice on their phone. They may open Today in a quiet morning moment, during a midday check-in, or after finishing a reading at night. The primary job is not to manage a dashboard; it is to receive, continue, reflect on, and return to a personalized devotional rhythm.

### Use Cases
- See what today's personalized devotional is and take the next step quickly.
- Continue or reveal a generated daily reading.
- Understand reading progress without turning faith into productivity analytics.
- Reflect with the Companion or journal after reading.
- Feel streak/ember completion as a gentle signal of rhythm and faithfulness.
- Start a new series when the current journey is complete or no active series exists.

### Brand Personality
Warm, sacred, personal. The interface should feel like a calm editorial devotional companion, not a generic AI wellness dashboard. It can be cinematic and premium, but it should stay humble, legible, and spiritually specific.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark warm near-black foundation with cream text and restrained warm-gold accent.
- Sacred editorial composition: one clear devotional focal point, generous spacing, quiet hierarchy, meaningful motion.
- Soft completion ember glow after reading, used as state feedback rather than decoration.
- Existing bottom navigation/menu remains untouched: Today, Bible, Companion, Journal.

### Anti-References
- AI-slop dashboards: over-carded layouts, neon purple/cyan gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, random metrics, generic “AI-powered” badges, hollow motivational copy.
- Over-gamified streak UI where freezes, dots, or stats become more important than prayer, scripture, and reflection.
- Flippant chatbot/wellness copy that clashes with Christian devotional tone.

### Design Principles
1. Make Today a daily liturgy, not a dashboard.
2. Lead with scripture, series context, and one clear next action.
3. Use glow, particles, and motion only when they explain state, especially completion.
4. Reframe streaks as rhythm/faithfulness, visually secondary to devotional content.
5. Let the Companion feel relational and pastoral, not like a notification widget.
6. Keep copy short, specific, and human; avoid generic wellness/AI filler.
7. Preserve bottom navigation/menu and release-critical product flows while improving the Today content stack.
