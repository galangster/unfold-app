## Description

Unfold is the world's most personal Bible experience. Unfold helps you meet God in Scripture every day through personalized Bible study, daily devotionals, and prayer journaling.

Whether you're building a quiet-time habit or looking for a deeper, more personal way to engage with God's Word, Unfold gives you a beautiful place to read, reflect, and respond.

FEATURES

PERSONALIZED DAILY DEVOTIONALS
• Receive daily devotional content shaped around your spiritual journey
• Reflect on Scripture with thoughtful prompts and guided structure
• Build a more consistent quiet-time rhythm

BIBLE STUDY + SCRIPTURE READING
• Read Scripture in a clean, distraction-free interface
• Highlight meaningful verses and revisit them later
• Stay anchored in God's Word throughout the day

PRAYER JOURNALING
• Capture prayers, reflections, and spiritual milestones
• Keep a private journal for your conversations with God
• Build a record of growth, gratitude, and answered prayer

QUIET-TIME HABITS
• Use reminders and streaks to stay consistent
• Return each day to your devotional, journal, and reading flow
• Create a calmer, more intentional spiritual routine

WHY UNFOLD
• Beautiful design focused on stillness, clarity, and reflection
• No ads, no clutter, and a reading experience built for focus
• Designed to support spiritual reflection, not shallow scrolling

Start your day with Scripture, deepen your reflection, and build a beautiful daily rhythm with God in Unfold.
