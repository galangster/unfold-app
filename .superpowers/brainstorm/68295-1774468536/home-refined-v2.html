The Problem We're Solving
The current home screen has 10+ conditional components stacking vertically:
Bridge card, RememberThis, midday check-in, evening wind-down, resume card, hero journey card, YourSeries, Day 1 review, StreakBox, PremiumNudge, HomeOnboardingTooltips, StreakCelebration.

This creates two problems:
1. The hero card can be pushed way down the screen by conditional content above it
2. Users scroll past a wall of cards to find what they came for

Solution: Consolidate into 6 fixed zones. Contextual content rotates within Zone 2 instead of stacking.

Zone 2 — Context Slot Priority

2

3

4
Daily Bridge — default AM message (Day 2+)
5
Nothing — zone collapses if none apply (Day 1 morning)
What We're Removing / Relocating
StreakBox (big version) → Condensed into Zone 4 small card. Full streak settings still accessible from You tab + tapping the card.

Day 1 Review → Move to a subtle prompt inside the reading completion flow instead of cluttering home.

PremiumNudgeCard → Keep as-is (it's already conditional + dismissable), but push to bottom or integrate into streak card as "Unlock freeze" CTA.

HomeOnboardingTooltips → Keep (first-time only, overlay system — doesn't affect layout).
Ambient Line Art — The Endel Signature
Two layers of ambient geometry:

Background layer — Full-screen concentric circles (2-3% opacity). Slowly rotating (0.3rpm). Positioned top-right and bottom-left for asymmetry. Uses react-native-skia Path + useClockValue for smooth rotation.

Card layer — Smaller circles inside Zone 3 hero card and Zone 4 grid cards (3-4% opacity). These are static or very slowly breathing. They make each card feel like it has depth without being "decorated."

GoldEmberField — Stays as-is (particles only after reading completion). The embers are the reward; the circles are the atmosphere.

Theme-reactive — All strokes use the accent color, so they shift with the user's chosen theme (gold, ocean, rose, etc).
