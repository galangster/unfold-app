A. Inline Bar

Compact player sits between the title and body text. No sheet, no overlay. Play/pause, progress, speed. Scrolls with content or stays pinned at top.

Pros

  • Always visible while reading
  • No modal interruption
  • Simplest implementation

Cons

  • No skip controls
  • Takes vertical space

B. Floating Bar

Glass-effect bar floats at the bottom of the reading screen. Shows play/pause, skip ±10s, time, speed. Content scrolls freely behind it.

Pros

  • Always accessible
  • Skip controls included
  • Doesn't interrupt reading
  • Feels native/modern

Cons

  • Overlaps bottom content
  • Needs safe area handling

C. FAB + Expand

Floating action button in bottom-right with circular progress ring. Tap to play/pause. Long-press or swipe up to reveal full controls (skip, speed, scrubber).

Pros

  • Minimal footprint
  • Progress visible at a glance
  • Doesn't cover content
  • Feels premium

Cons

  • Controls hidden by default
  • Discoverability of expand gesture
  • More complex interaction
