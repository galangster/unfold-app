Where does the "restore player" indicator live?

When the mini player is hidden but audio is still playing, the user needs a way to bring it back. Where should this indicator appear?

Context

Audio plays across all tabs and even when the app is backgrounded. The indicator needs to be visible everywhere — not just the reading screen.

A

Floating pill above tab bar

A small floating pill (like Spotify's green "now playing" bar) sits just above the tab bar. Shows title + play/pause. Tap to expand back to full mini player. Visible on every tab.

B

Pulsing icon in tab bar

A small pulsing audio wave icon appears in/near the tab bar (or replaces one of the tab icons temporarily). Subtle but persistent. Tap to restore the full mini player.

C

Collapsed mini bar (thinner version of current player)

Instead of fully hiding, the player collapses to a thin strip — just the progress bar + a tap target. Like Apple Music's collapsed state. Always visible above the tab bar, minimal footprint.

D

No in-app indicator — rely on system controls

When hidden, the player is only accessible via iOS lock screen / Control Center. The app itself has no persistent indicator. Clean but less discoverable.

Pros

  • Zero visual clutter
  • Familiar iOS pattern

Cons

  • Users may not know audio is still playing
  • Hard to bring back the in-app player
