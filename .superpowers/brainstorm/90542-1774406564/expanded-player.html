Should tapping the mini player expand to a full-screen view?

Currently the mini bar is the only player UI. With longer devotionals (up to 30 min), a richer player view might be valuable.

A

Yes — Full-screen expanded player

Tap the mini bar or pill to open a full-screen player. Shows: artwork/theme image, title, scrubber/progress bar, play/pause, skip -10s/+10s, speed picker, close. Like Apple Music or Spotify's expanded view.

B

No — Mini bar is enough

The mini bar already has play/pause, speed, and time display. For devotionals, that's sufficient. Skip buttons and scrubber aren't worth the complexity. Keep it simple.

Pros

  • Less code
  • Never blocks the reading screen
  • YAGNI — most devotionals are 2-3 min

Cons

  • No scrubber for seeking in long audio
  • No skip buttons
  • Speed cycling is the only control
C

Half-sheet — expand mini bar into a bottom sheet

Tap the mini bar to expand into a half-height bottom sheet (not full screen). Shows scrubber, skip buttons, speed picker. Can still see the reading screen behind it. Swipe down to collapse back to mini bar.
